## About Me

Hello! I am a Ph.D. student at KAIST AI, co-advised by [Alice Oh](https://aliceoh9.github.io/) and [Minjoon Seo](https://seominjoon.github.io/). I am currently an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon Web Services (AWS), working on synthetic data and post-training for tool-use agents.

My research focuses on LLMs, with a current emphasis on tool-use agents. I have also worked on multilinguality, reasoning models, and evaluation, and I am interested in speech understanding and generation and in text–speech foundation models (omni-models). Please feel free to reach out if you’d like to discuss anything 🙂
